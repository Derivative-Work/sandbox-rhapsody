Rules applied by the script:
- **shift**: the track's constant offset is applied when events are exported (`timing/events/`). Every percussive part runs 12–26 ms early; soft attacks (strings +33, brass +53) are shifted to their audible attack, which is where a visible hit has to land.
- **ghosts** (onset in stem silence below −50 dBFS) are dropped from the exported events; the counts are in each event file.
- **stem**: Percussion, FX and Brass drive their machines from the stem's own detected onsets (`timing/stem_events/`), because their MIDI holds 0%, 9% and 18% of what is heard. Brass is a sustained timbre but is played as stabs, and its MIDI misses the biggest ones (`timing/midi_check.png`, 213.75 s and 216.35 s); its MIDI pitches stay available for which-button-is-pressed, its timing comes from the stem.
- **ignore**: misattributed or unvalidatable fragments: drums read from consonants in the vocal stem, a 49-note sawtooth in the vocal coda, 36 electric-piano notes in the drum stem, 5 percussion notes.
- The vocal tracks pass on pitch and loose timing only; vents follow the vocal stem's envelope and captions follow forced alignment (step 5), not these notes.
