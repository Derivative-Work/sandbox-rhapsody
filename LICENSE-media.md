## What this covers

- The video in every cut: the main video, the four vertical shorts, the per-set reels, the animatics and the drafts.
- The music: the Suno V6 track "It Was Just a Benchmark", its stems and MIDI, the mastered track, the card-section
  bounce, and the rejected takes.
- The lyrics, captions (`deliverables/*.srt`, `deliverables/shorts/*.srt`) and thumbnail.
- Rendered plates, overlay tracks, frame packs and stills under `renders/` and `archive/`.
- The production record, written by the agent: the brief, logs, prompts, style spec, shot list, timing map, the
  upload metadata, and the other documents under `docs/`, `log/`, `style/`, `shots/`, `timing/` and `lyrics/`.
